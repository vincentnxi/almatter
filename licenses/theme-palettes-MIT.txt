Named theme palettes
====================

Seven of the named themes in Almatter's settings (Catppuccin, Catppuccin
Latte, Nord, Rosé Pine, Rosé Pine Moon, Rosé Pine Dawn, Tokyo Night) use the
colour values of the shared palettes of fastframe-theme, which adapts the
original colour schemes listed below. The values are copied into
app/Almatter.App/Models/NamedThemes.cs. Each project is under the MIT License,
reproduced once per copyright holder below. For readability, the
secondary text and accent colours of Catppuccin Latte and Rosé Pine Dawn are
slightly darkened, and the secondary text of Tokyo Night and Rosé Pine Moon
slightly lightened; every other value is as published.
