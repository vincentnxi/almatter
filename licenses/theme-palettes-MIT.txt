Named theme palettes
====================

The Nord theme in Almatter's settings uses the colour values of the Nord
palette of fastframe-theme, which adapts the original Nord colour scheme.
The values are copied into app/Almatter.App/Models/NamedThemes.cs, as
published. Both projects are under the MIT License, reproduced once per
copyright holder below.

- fastframe-theme  https://github.com/crmne/fastframe
  Copyright (c) 2026 Carmine Paolino
- Nord             https://github.com/nordtheme/nord
  Copyright (c) 2016-present Sven Greb <development@svengreb.de> (https://www.svengreb.de)

The other named themes (Aube, Crépuscule, Salon, Cardamome, Crème) were made
for Almatter and are under its own licence (LICENSE at the root of the
repository).
